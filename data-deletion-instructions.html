User Data Deletion Instructions

You maintain full sovereignty over your data in Odin CMS. This page provides clear, actionable instructions on how to disconnect your Google account, revoke OAuth permissions, delete stored tokens, and request permanent erasure of your data.

1. Data Deletion Overview

Odin CMS respects your privacy and complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, EU/UK GDPR, California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023.

When you connect your Google Account (for YouTube, Google Analytics 4, or Google Search Console) to Odin CMS, we only access the minimal scopes required to deliver your requested publishing and analytics features. The same applies if you connect X, LinkedIn, or Threads for publishing: we only request the permissions needed to publish what you choose to the connected account. You retain the absolute right to terminate any of these connections and permanently purge all associated tokens and cached metrics at any time.

2. Method 1: Self-Service Disconnection in Odin CMS (Instant Token Wipe)

Any workspace administrator or authorized editor can instantly disconnect a Google integration directly within the Odin CMS user interface:

1

Open Workspace Settings

Log in to your Odin CMS dashboard at your publication's URL and click on Settings in the left navigation sidebar.

2

Navigate to Integrations

Select the Integrations tab to view all connected distribution and analytics channels.

3

Locate the Connected Integration

Find the integration card for YouTube, Google Analytics 4, Google Search Console, X, LinkedIn, or Threads. Active connections will display a green "Connected" badge with channel or property details.

4

Click Disconnect

Click the red Disconnect button and confirm your choice in the confirmation dialog.

What Happens Automatically Upon In-App Disconnect

When you click Disconnect, Odin CMS executes the following actions immediately:

  • Token Erasure: The stored accessToken and refreshToken are immediately wiped from our production database.
  • Metadata Clearing: Saved property IDs, channel labels, and configuration metadata are reset to empty.
  • Cache Invalidation: Provider-specific cached analytics data in our Redis memory layer (e.g., GA4 property mappings, Search Console error logs, and YouTube video cache counters) is invalidated immediately, and transient operational entries expire automatically via their short TTLs.
  • Proactive Google Revocation: Our backend issues an asynchronous HTTP call to Google's OAuth revocation endpoint (https://oauth2.googleapis.com/revoke) to inform Google's servers that the grant has been terminated.
  • X, LinkedIn & Threads: The stored tokens, account ID, name, username, and profile details are wiped immediately. Odin CMS does not call a revocation endpoint on these platforms, so also remove Odin CMS in the platform's settings (see Method 2 below). Posts already published remain on the platform and can be deleted there.
  • Status Update: The integration status transitions to DISCONNECTED, preventing any further API calls.

3. Method 2: Revoke Access via Your Google, X, LinkedIn, or Threads Account

You do not need to rely on Odin CMS to revoke access. You can independently revoke Odin CMS's permissions directly from your personal or enterprise Google Account:

1

Open Google Account Permissions

Open a web browser and navigate directly to https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

2

Find Odin CMS

Scroll through the list of "Third-party apps with account access" and select Odin CMS.

3

Click "Remove Access"

Click the Remove Access button and confirm when prompted by Google.

Result: Google's authentication servers immediately invalidate all access tokens and refresh tokens associated with Odin CMS. Any subsequent API request made by Odin CMS will fail with an HTTP 401 Unauthorized status, and our platform will mark the channel as reconnect_required or disconnected.

Revoking X, LinkedIn, or Threads directly

To remove Odin CMS's access from inside the platform (recommended in addition to disconnecting in Odin CMS):

X

X (formerly Twitter)

Open x.com/settings/connected_apps, select Odin CMS, and choose Revoke app permissions.

in

LinkedIn

Open linkedin.com/psettings/permitted-services (Settings & Privacy → Data privacy → Permitted services), select Odin CMS, and choose Remove.

@

Threads

In your Threads or Instagram account settings, open the connected apps / website permissions list (Threads: Settings → Account → Website permissions), select Odin CMS, and remove it. Menu names can differ slightly between app versions.

Result: The platform invalidates the access Odin CMS was granted. Odin CMS can no longer publish to that account, and the integration card will show it needs to be reconnected.

4. Method 3: Permanently Delete the Integration Record

If you wish to remove the integration record completely rather than leaving it in a disconnected state:

  • Workspace administrators can click Delete Integration Permanently in the integration settings modal, or submit a request via our API endpoint DELETE /v1/integrations/delete/:propertyId/:provider.
  • This performs a hard database delete (deleteOne) that completely removes the document from the MongoDB integrations collection.

5. Method 4: Complete Account & Workspace Data Deletion Request

If your organization is closing its Odin CMS subscription or if you are an individual user requesting complete erasure under GDPR, CCPA, or DPDP:

How to Submit a Deletion Request

Send an email to our Data Protection Officer with the details below:

  • Recipient: connect@odincms.com and ashok@astconsulting.in
  • Subject Line: Data Deletion Request - [Your Organization or Publication Name]
  • Required Details:
    1. Your registered administrator email address;
    2. Publication name and domain hosted on Odin CMS;
    3. Specification of deletion scope: (A) Google integration tokens only, or (B) Entire publication workspace, articles, media, and user accounts.

Timeline: We verify the requester's authority within 48 hours and execute the permanent purging of all active production records within 30 calendar days. You will receive an official written Confirmation of Erasure upon completion.

6. What Data Is Deleted vs Retained

Data Category On In-App Disconnect On Full Account Deletion Request
OAuth Access & Refresh Tokens Purged immediately (0 seconds) Purged immediately (0 seconds)
X, LinkedIn & Threads Tokens and Account Details Purged immediately (0 seconds) Purged immediately (0 seconds)
Posts Already Published to X, LinkedIn, or Threads Not deleted by Odin CMS; they remain on the platform until you delete them there Not deleted by Odin CMS; they remain on the platform until you delete them there. Our publishing audit-log entries are deleted within 30 days
Cached Analytics (Redis) Provider keys invalidated immediately; transient caches expire via TTL (5m – 24h) Purged immediately (0 seconds)
Historical Performance Snapshots (MongoDB) Data collection halts immediately; historical records purge via MongoDB TTL indexes (14 to 400 days) Permanently purged within 30 days
Integration Metadata (Channel / Property ID) Reset to empty immediately Permanently removed from database
User Profile & Login Records Retained (so you can continue using CMS) Permanently deleted within 30 days
Article Content & Media Assets Retained (editorial archive remains intact) Exported to customer upon request, then permanently deleted
Disaster Recovery Backups Overwritten within 30 days Overwritten within 30 days via rolling rotation
