9.2 How to Disconnect Google Integrations in Odin CMS

Any workspace administrator can disconnect connected Google services (YouTube, Google Analytics, or Google Search Console) instantly:

1

Navigate to Settings

Log in to your Odin CMS workspace and click Settings in the main navigation sidebar.

2

Go to Integrations

Select the Integrations tab to view all active third-party connections.

3

Click Disconnect

Find the Google service and click the red Disconnect button. Confirm your choice in the dialog.

Automatic Disconnection Effects

When an integration is disconnected:

  • Stored OAuth accessToken and refreshToken are wiped from our active databases immediately (0 seconds).
  • Provider-specific cached analytics records in Redis memory are invalidated immediately.
  • Our backend makes a proactive call to Google's token revocation endpoint (https://oauth2.googleapis.com/revoke) to terminate the authorization grant.
  • The integration is placed in DISCONNECTED status, ceasing all automated synchronization.

9.3 Independent Revocation via Google Account

You can independently revoke Odin CMS's access to your Google account at any time without accessing our platform. Visit https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, select Odin CMS, and click Remove Access. Google will immediately invalidate all issued tokens.
